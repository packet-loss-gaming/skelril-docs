# Sky Wars

A feather fight in the sky over the Vineam district (`/warp SKY-WARS`). Use the lobby door to join or leave. Joining saves
your gear and gives you a **Sky Feather [Standard]**. You get everything back when you leave or are knocked out.

## Starting

Everyone in the game stands on a coloured glass deck in the lobby. White is free-for-all. Orange, yellow, lime, gray, purple,
blue, green, red and black are teams. Light blue, magenta, pink, light gray, cyan and brown give no team. At least two players
are needed, and not all on one team. After a countdown on the start pad, everyone is launched into the sky.

**Flag signs** in the lobby change the next game: **Regen Enabled** (slow healing), **Chicken++** (more chickens),
**Flammable** (push-backs set players on fire), **Doom** (everyone starts with a Doom feather), **Acid++** (water hurts three
times as much) and **64 Click** (feathers have 64 uses instead of infinite). Flags can only change in the lobby.

## Sky Feathers

- **Left-click** flies in the direction you look.
- **Right-click** fires a flame line along your view. Players near the line are pushed away and lose their flight for a
  moment. Chickens on the line become **power-ups**.

| Feather | Radius | Flight | Push back | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 | 2 | 2 | Infinite |
| Flight | 3 | 6 | 4 | 5 |
| Push Back | 5 | 2 | 6 | 5 |
| Doom | 7 | 6 | 6 | Infinite (64 with 64 Click) |

Power-ups:

- **Book o' Omens**: slams every enemy down.
- **Frost Orb**: 16 snowballs. A hit stops the target's flight for 4 to 8 seconds.
- **Defroster**: resets your own cooldowns.
- Flight and Push Back feathers.
- Rarely, the **Infinite Doom** feather (it replaces your inventory and everyone is warned).

## Rules

There is no fall damage, your food stays full, and you only heal through Regen Enabled. Water hurts. Leaving the lobby and arena
takes you out. The last player or team in the sky wins. Each point pays 1.5 Skrin, and a victory is worth 100 points. Wins count
towards the Sky Wars high score. Only chat commands work, and guild powers stay off. Punch the skull by the lobby to watch a
game.
