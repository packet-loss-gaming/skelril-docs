# Spleef

Six ice arenas in the Glacies Mare district. Stand on the floor, dig out the ice under your opponents and be the last
one standing.

`/spleef` lists the arenas, how many players are on each floor, and whether the walls are up.

Standing on an arena floor puts you in the game: your gear is saved, and you get a diamond shovel and a full
stomach. Falling through a hole, yours or someone else's, takes you out and gives your items back.

Once two or more players are on the floor, standing without digging earns a warning and then a punishment: the snow
around you is removed.

Your [guild powers](/guide/guilds) switch off while you play and return when you leave.
