# Jungle Raid

A team battle in the Carpe Diem jungle arena (`/warp JUNGLE-RAID`). Use the lobby door to join or leave. Joining saves your
gear, takes your flight, and gives you the Bandit kit. You get everything back when you leave or are knocked out.

## Getting ready

- **Class signs**: left-click a class to switch to its kit.
- **Mode sign**: each click cycles **Selection** (pick a class), **Random** (a class is assigned at the start), **Survival**
  (no kit: mine ore and hunt animals for what you need) and **Scavenger** (each kit is hidden in three chests in the arena).
- **Flag signs**: left-click a flag to turn it on or off. Flags can only change in the lobby.

To start, at least two players stand on the lobby's concrete: **white** for free-for-all, **blue** or **red** for a team. If
everyone is on the same side, the game does not start.

| Class | Gear |
|---|---|
| Bandit | Iron sword, bow, 128 arrows, 96 TNT |
| Archer | Bow with Punch II, bow with Flame II, 128 arrows, 96 TNT |
| Knight | Iron sword with Fire Aspect II and Knockback II, 96 TNT |
| Lumberjack | Diamond axe with Sharpness III and Knockback II |
| Sniper | The **Legendary Jungle Bow**, wooden sword, 32 arrows |
| Engineer | Iron sword, diamond pickaxe, 348 TNT |

Every class also gets shears, 64 cooked beef and a compass. All classes except the Lumberjack get a stronger iron axe. Bandit,
Archer, Knight and Engineer also get flint and steel. You wear leather armour in your team's colour.

## The fight

Everyone starts at a random spot in the jungle. A one-minute calm comes first: click your compass to move to another
spot. Then the countdown, and the slaughter begins. The last player or team standing wins.

- Your compass lists the enemies and their distance. Dark red names are in your line of sight.
- Enemies only see the name above your head while they can see you; leaves and walls hide it. Your team mates always see it.
- Falling onto jungle leaves bounces you instead of hurting.
- Leaving the lobby and arena takes you out. After 15 minutes, TNT starts raining (unless **No Time Limit** is on).
- The Legendary Jungle Bow throws extra harming or poison potions at targets you hit from far away.
- Your guild powers stay off unless **Allow Guilds** is on. Only chat commands work during a game.

| Points | |
|---|---|
| Player killed | 25 |
| Long shot (more than 25 nodes) | 25 |
| Epic long shot (more than 75 nodes) | 50 |
| Titan killed | 75 |
| Victory | 100 |

Each point pays 1.5 Skrin to the winners. Knocked-out players get half, paid when they are out. Big scores sometimes turn up a jungle artifact
for extra Skrin. Wins count towards the Jungle Raid high score.

## Flags

| Flag | Effect |
|---|---|
| Trampoline | With Super, every fall bounces. |
| Super | Bigger explosions, faster rain. |
| Titan Mode | One player is the titan: night vision, double melee damage, instant block breaking. Killing the titan makes you the titan. |
| No Chill | No calm minute before the fight. |
| Explosive Arrows | Arrows explode where they land. |
| Grenades | Snowballs fall from the sky and explode when thrown. |
| Torment Arrows | Landing arrows hurt everything nearby and sometimes heal the shooter. |
| Death Touch | Any hit kills. |
| End of Days | TNT rain from the start. |
| Potion Plummet | Splash potions rain down. |
| No Fire Spread | Fire does not spread. |
| No Mining | Stone, dirt and grass cannot be dug. |
| No Block Break | No block can be dug by hand. |
| No Time Limit | No sudden death. |
| Allow Guilds | Guild powers stay on. |
| Random Rockets | Fireworks go off on random players. |
| Enhanced Compass | The compass lists enemies (on by default). |
| Death Rockets | Fireworks where a player is knocked out (on by default). |

After a round the arena restores itself. The lobby opens again when it is done. Punch the skull by the lobby to watch a game
as a spectator.
