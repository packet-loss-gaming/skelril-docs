# The Factory

The Factory (Oblitus district, the old factory) mass-produces for anyone who brings the raw materials. Throw them in yourself: the
factory knows who threw what. It only works while you stand inside it, and it makes up to nine items every four seconds for each
player there, picked at random from everyone's orders. What it makes is yours alone (only you can pick it up). Orders are kept when
you leave and continue when you come back.

## Smelting

Throw iron, copper or gold ore, raw iron, copper or gold, or their raw blocks into the hopper while you are online. Copper ore
counts 3, a raw block 9, and the Tome of Cursed Smelting can triple what you put in. You get **four ingots** for each one, poured out
on the smelter tracks. Anything else you throw into the hopper is destroyed.

## Brewing

Throw into a vat glass bottles, nether wart and one base ingredient:

| Ingredient | Potion |
| --- | --- |
| Magma cream | Fire resistance |
| Sugar | Swiftness |
| Glistering melon | Healing |
| Spider eye | Poison |
| Ghast tear | Regeneration |
| Blaze powder | Strength |
| Golden carrot | Night vision |
| Pufferfish | Water breathing |

A fermented spider eye turns the first six into slowness, harming or weakness. Add redstone for a longer potion (not healing or
harming), glowstone for a stronger one, gunpowder for splash potions and nether quartz for more. Each set of 3 bottles, 1 nether
wart and one of every other ingredient makes **6 potions** (9 with quartz); they come up the fountain. Put every ingredient for one
potion in the same vat. Do not jump into a vat: nothing living survives it.

## Lava supply

Ingots need lava in the furnace pool. The pipes refill it, but now and then a pipe block breaks (dropping iron). Place iron blocks
to repair the pipes; iron blocks are the only thing you can place in the factory. Drowned climb out of the fountain while it works.

Flying players take double damage from arrows and other projectiles there. Tested only without a game client so far.
