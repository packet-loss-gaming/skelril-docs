# Enchanted Forest

Use `/warp enchanted-forest` to reach the forest in Carpe Diem. Harvest logs for experience and up to three pieces of loot. Each log takes a little hunger or saturation. Watch for fairy tricks, including poison, scattered inventory, and temporary tooth loss.

Clear grass, ferns and flowers for a chance to find spawn eggs or meet a rabbit. If the Killer Bunny appears, move away from the grass. You can fill a bucket in the forest. Flight is disabled, and you need building access to place blocks.

Logs and leaves grow back after three hours. Shrubs and collected water return after eight minutes.
