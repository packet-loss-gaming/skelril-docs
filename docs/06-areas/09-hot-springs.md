# Hot Springs

The City has hot springs in Carpe Diem and Glacies Mare. Visit to clear nausea, blindness, weakness, poison and slowness. Either spring grants Regeneration III for five minutes and Speed II and Jump Boost II for three minutes. The Tome of Divinity doubles those durations.

The lapis geyser at Carpe Diem launches you upward and protects you from the landing. Steer toward a solid surface above Y 70 to leave the geyser.

Zombies can emerge from the pools during thunderstorms.
