# Gold Rush

Right-click a **Play Gold Rush** sign near the Vineam bank. Bring an empty inventory and at least 100 Skrin. Press the button in the lobby to start a seven-minute robbery.

Find the red and blue wool keys in the first room's chests. Use each key on its matching lock sign. In the next room, watch the flashes below the levers and match the pattern before the water rises. Carrying a Phantom Hymn changes the flood to lava.

Right-click the treasury chest to escape. Your split goes to your wallet and the robbery updates your Gold Rush scores. Death during the robbery costs at least 100 Skrin or 0.5% of your balance, whichever is greater, up to the amount you have. Logging out during a robbery triggers the alarm when you return.
