# The Grave Yard

The Grave Yard (Carpe Diem district) is where the dead of the City rest, and a dungeon under and around the old cemetery. Here you
recover lost belongings, fight for loot in the temple, or farm the zombies that rise at night.

You cannot fly anywhere in the Grave Yard: your `fly` privilege is taken away while you are inside and given back when you leave
(creative players and spectators are not affected). Sheep that try to graze in the Grave Yard explode, and its grass never spreads.

## Headstones

Players who die in the City without a Gem of Life are buried here. Each grave is a chest under a headstone carrying the date, "RIP"
and the player's name. See [Dying](/guide/mechanics/death#graves) for what goes in and how long it is protected.

## Night hauntings

At night, zombies rise from graves you linger near, more the longer you stay. Grave Zombies carry Gems of Life, Phantom items and
rare weapons, so they are a way for new players to make money, but it is easy to be overwhelmed.

## The temple

Bring a pickaxe and either a **Gem of Darkness** or the **Ancient Crown**: in the dark temple you are blinded without one. A Gem of
Life is advisable too. Inside the temple zombies rise around you, ordinary teleports cannot reach you, your prayers fall silent,
Master weapons recharge twice as fast, and a hit passes your potion effects on to whatever you strike.

* **The staircase** spirals down into the depths. Its steps crumble beneath your feet, and cave spiders infest it (they die in
  daylight).
* **The lever room** comes before a hall of pressure plates that throw harming, poison and weakness potions. Set the levers to the
  hidden pattern and everyone in the temple hears "You hear a clicking sound.": the plates are safe.
* **The hall of lights** flips between normal and soul fire. Soul fire slows you and burns anyone who strikes while standing in it,
  and the hall's zombies hit hard enough to throw you across it. Your guild powers do not work here.
* **The creeper maze** ends at a wall of cracked stone bricks that you break with a pickaxe. Using a **Phantom Hymn** with 10
  Phantom Essence lets a spirit carry you to the maze's end.
* **The parkour** crosses cracked-brick paths that crumble under you. The paths are rebuilt every hour, so do not jump on the hour.
  Guild powers do not work here either.
* **The rewards room** locks its door while anyone is inside and refills its chest with loot when it is empty again. Guardian
  Zombies in ancient armour guard it, more and stronger the longer you stay, and every blow from them shortens your good potions.
  Its small sacrificial pit makes [repairs](/guide/mechanics/sacrificial-pits#repairs) twice as strong and pays more for Phantom
  Gold and Phantom Diamonds. Its Nether portal takes you back to a headstone, with up to five extra pieces of loot.

Full ancient armour can burst with energy when struck (for players only in a thunderstorm): it heals the wearer and their kind and
hurls everything else away in flames.

## Watching

Punch one of the three skeleton skulls (on the temple and at the north and south ends) to watch the Grave Yard as an invisible
spectator. Your belongings are kept safe meanwhile, only other spectators can see you, and nothing in the world reacts to you. You
float where you appear (press K to fly, H to pass through walls and E to fly faster) but touch nothing, nothing can hurt or burn you
and you don't get hungry (no health, food or item bar). You are put back by the skull as soon as you leave the Grave Yard.
Spectating has only been tested without a game client so far.
