# Halzeil

Halzeil is Skelril's current ranged world. Enter from a cobblestone Range Gate or use `/warp HALZEIL`. You can
build in both Halzeil and its Nether, subject to local protection. Halzeil extends roughly 5,000 blocks
each way from the centre of the world.

Ranged worlds share these rules.

- In range overworlds, saplings that sit on suitable soil for over a minute can plant themselves if
  the spot is clear and unprotected. The rest of the stack stays on the ground.
- Explosions in either range dimension still hurt nearby creatures and players. Above relative Y 58,
  explosions from hostile mobs such as creepers and ghasts leave blocks intact and start no fires.
  Your own TNT and guild explosives work everywhere you could build, so you can quarry with TNT.
- Natural hostile mobs are less likely to appear around well-lit spots. Nether portals in the range
  worlds and Astaria suppress portal mob spawns.
- On hard difficulty, a zombie that gets through a wooden door opens both halves if the door is unprotected.
- Landing many more hits on hostile mobs than you take within five minutes can summon endermites.
  The swarm starts at the mob and reaches you one endermite at a time. Endermite hits bypass armour
  and can poison you. Killing one near a recent swarm can cause an explosion.

## World levels

Your world level is shared between Halzeil and its Nether. Right-click a block with Demonic Ashes
to raise it by one. Each death in a range world halves your level, rounded down to a minimum of one.
Wearing all four Peaceful Warrior pieces lowers it by one every five seconds. Sacrificing Barbarian
Bones trades one level per bone for the same number of ashes, down to level one.

Deeper mining can turn up ashes. From level two, breaking natural ore can start an ore pool that
drops items reserved for you over several seconds. Mining at higher levels earns more block XP,
but can attract monsters or infest nearby stone with silverfish.

Your level determines how much damage you deal to ordinary range monsters and how hard they can
hit you. While your level is above one, it shows beside your potion effects in the top right corner
whenever you are in Halzeil or its Nether. At higher levels, your
share of the fight determines the Demonic Runes you earn. Sacrificing a rune opens a temporary
portal that releases protected loot. While you carry a rune, warps, pearls, world gates and portable
containers are restricted. Nether portals still work.

At level ten or higher, Fangz, Fear Knight or Storm Bringer can appear in a dark spot out of sight.
They usually target players at or above their level. A lower-level player who attacks can draw
retaliation.
