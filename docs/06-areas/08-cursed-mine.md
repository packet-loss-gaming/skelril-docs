# The Cursed Mine

Bring a pickaxe to the mine in the Oblitus district of the City. Ore yields extra resources and experience, and regrows after about 30 seconds. The mine can drain valuables from inventories and dropped piles every few seconds. Named items are exempt from the drain.

Press a stone button or cross the tripwire passage to open the flood gate briefly. The deepest part of the mine holds emeralds and is patrolled by blazes. Holy prayers, flight, crafting and ordinary building are restricted inside.

Mining can summon a ghost. Some bring gifts. Others put you on Dave's hit list, which blocks warps, portals and pearls. Your unrestored blocks can also block travel. Quitting in the mine while your blocks are unrestored puts you on the hit list for ten minutes, and you die if you reconnect while it still has your name.

The mine takes drainable valuables on death, even if a Gem of Life saves the rest of your inventory. When at least two players are in the mine, punch the skeleton skull to spectate.
