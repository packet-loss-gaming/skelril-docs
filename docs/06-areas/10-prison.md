# Vineam Prison

Use `/warp VINEAM-PRISON` to reach the entrance. Find your way through the prison and right-click the chest in the office. The raid pays gold and bonus loot, then sends you back to the entrance. You can run the maze again to claim another reward.

Flight, guild powers and holy prayers are suspended inside. A warp into the prison takes you to the entrance. Pearls and other teleports between rooms are blocked.
