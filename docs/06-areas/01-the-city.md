# The City

The City is the original Grindstone world converted block by block: its terrain, 238 regions, signs, item frames and
chest contents. It is a void world, so there is nothing beyond the built area. Your first stop is the spawn.

The City is protected: you can build and dig only in designated areas (see [Protecting your stuff](/guide/protecting-your-stuff)).
Hostile mobs do not spawn on their own in the City or the sky world, but the old mob spawners (the Grave Yard's spiders, among
others) work again. The City has no Nether.

## What was converted

Converted double chests keep both halves and their combined 54-slot inventory, including empty halves. Streams and waterfalls retain their flowing water.

Imported lanterns include standing and hanging variants, with warm normal lights and blue soul lights. Fountains use Minecraft-style water spreading.

Imported heavy and light weighted pressure plates preserve their analog redstone strength.

Iron trapdoors retain their open/closed state and work with redstone. Imported soul campfires retain their lit state and blue fire.
