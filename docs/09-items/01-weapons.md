# Weapons

## Bows

All ten custom bows can be drawn and fired. Hold the place/right-click control or zoom to draw, then release to shoot. Drawing uses each bow's pull-back artwork and preserves its identity, enchantments and wear. Arrow impacts respect safe-region PvP rules and dimension boundaries. Arrows retain the source bow's damage multiplier even if you switch weapons after firing.

Survival shots require ammunition and use durability. Infinity preserves regular arrows, while tipped arrows are consumed. Creative shots need neither ammunition nor durability. Switching items or dropping a charging bow cancels the draw.

## Melee weapons

Custom melee weapons apply their original damage multipliers, including fractional values, before armor reduction. Normal weapon and enchantment rules still apply.

Combat works like classic Minecraft (1.0): there is no attack charge, so every click hits at full strength and fast clicking is the way to fight. After you land a hit, that target ignores your further hits for half a second, unless a hit is stronger, in which case it deals only the difference. These follow-up hits cause no knockback and trigger no special attacks. The half-second applies to each attacker separately, so two players on one target each land their own hits. Critical hits (striking while falling) still apply. There are no sweep attacks. Axes, pickaxes and shovels hit for one, two and three points less than a sword of the same material.

Player-against-player combat follows the [PvP rules](/guide/protecting-your-stuff#player-combat).

## Special attacks

A hit with a special weapon on a surviving target may unleash one of its special attacks, picked at random. Swords and bows have
separate 3.8-second cooldowns. Special attacks respect PvP rules and dimension boundaries, and your chat tells you which one fired.

| Weapon | Special attacks |
| --- | --- |
| Master Sword, Master Bow | **Curse** (Wither III, longer the healthier you are, up to five minutes) or **Life Leech** (extra damage that heals you by the health it removes). |
| Fear Sword | Chain Lightning, Fear Blaze, Curse, Weaken, Decimate, Hell-cano, and against monsters Soul Smite. |
| Fear Bow | Curse, Magic Chain (slows), Fear Strike, Soul Reaper, Hell-cano and Disarm. While it is recharging, its arrows call down lightning where they land. |
| Unleashed Sword | Evil Focus, Healing Light, Speed, Regen, Doom Blade and Life Leech. |
| Unleashed Bow | Famine, Life Leech, Evil Focus, Speed, Glowing Fog and Surge. Its arrows pull nearby dropped items to you and set what is near them alight. |
| Phantom Sabre | **Phantom Fortune:** for a while the target sheds gold (only you can pick it up) as it loses health. |
| Red Sword, Red Bow | **Red Blight:** spends your redstone to strike the target and spread to its kind nearby. Needs redstone in your inventory. |
| Bat Bow, Chicken Bow | Arrows trail bats or chickens, and where they land a swarm of 125 appears for 30 seconds (once every 15 seconds). |

Some specials hurt you too: stand clear of your own Hell-cano. Guild members unlock two more: the Ninja
[Ignition](/guide/guilds/ninja) and the Rogue [Nightmare](/guide/guilds/rogue).

The **Executioner Axe** deals 7 damage times ten for every zombie within 7 blocks, and nothing at all when there are none. Only the first hit on a target in each half second counts. Every such hit that gets through the target's armor costs you 2 hearts, which your own armor does not reduce.
