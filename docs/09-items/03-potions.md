# Potions

## Combat potions

Drink with the place/right-click control. Survival drinking leaves an empty bottle; creative drinking keeps the potion. The recipes have fixed effects and durations:

| Potion | Effects |
| --- | --- |
| Divine | Regeneration II for 30 seconds; Absorption III, Strength II, Resistance II and Speed II for 5 minutes. |
| Holy | Regeneration II for 30 seconds; Absorption III, Strength II, Resistance II and Speed II for 1 minute. |
| Extreme | Absorption II, Strength I, Resistance I and Speed I for 5 minutes. |

The effects use Mineclonia's potion system. Its damage and movement formulas can differ from Minecraft's even when effect levels match.

## Travel potions

The **Phantom Potion** carries you to your most recent grave and the **Potion of Restitution** to where you last died; see
[Dying](/guide/mechanics/death#getting-back). Gifts such as the Newbie Phantom Potion expire after the time shown on them and stop
working.

Ordinary potions can be brewed in bulk at the [Factory](/guide/areas/factory#brewing).
