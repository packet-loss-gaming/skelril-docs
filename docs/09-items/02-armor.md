# Armour

Ancient, Ancient Royal (with the Ancient Crown), Nectric, Necros and God armor have stronger armor values than the material they are made of (in Minecraft their strength came from enchantments; here it is part of the item). A full Ancient set has 20 armor and 40 toughness; a full Nectric, Necros or God set has 23 armor and 44 toughness. Enchantments still apply on top.

Armor can block up to 92% of a hit, not Minecraft's 80%. Ordinary armor never reaches the old limit, so only these sets use the higher tier.

## Set bonuses

A full set requires all four armor slots. Ancient Royal pieces also count toward the Ancient set, and an Ancient Crown can replace the Royal helmet.

| Set | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient | Attacks repair a randomly chosen armor piece. |
| Ancient Royal | Ancient repair plus partial absorption of large hits. |
| Nectric | Partial absorption of large hits. |
| Necros | Attack-based repair, partial absorption of large hits, and **Necrosis**. |
| Peaceful Warrior | Outside the City, hostile mobs cannot target or hurt you, but striking one costs you 12 damage. Apocalyptic zombies ignore you everywhere. |

Repair is stronger when attacking another player.

**Necrosis:** a player who hits a Necros wearer may be cursed ("Taste necrosis!") with a few seconds of drain that heals the wearer;
the more hurt the wearer, the likelier it is. A monster's hit triggers a death strike one time in five: the wearer is healed and
nearby monsters are struck.

In the [Grave Yard](/guide/areas/graveyard), full ancient armour can also burst with energy when struck.

## Ancient Crown

An equipped **Ancient Crown** also multiplies ordinary healing by 2.5, doubles vanilla XP gained after Mending has taken its share, and automatically compacts gold (nuggets into ingots, ingots into blocks) after pickups. It protects you from the Grave Yard's blindness like a Gem of Darkness. Carrying an **Imbued Crystal** compacts gold the same way. Direct health transfers such as Life Leech use their own amount. The XP bonus applies to vanilla experience, not guild experience.

## Red Feather

A **Red Feather** in your inventory spends redstone from your inventory to absorb damage, then needs time to recharge (longer after
a bigger hit). It does not block magic damage such as poison and wither.
