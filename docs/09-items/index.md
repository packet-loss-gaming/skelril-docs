# Items

All 125 Grindstone custom items are registered with their own artwork. Most have their abilities; the remaining tools, scrolls,
tomes, hymns and party boxes are on the [roadmap](/roadmap).

| Page | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Weapons](/guide/items/weapons) | Custom bows and swords, combat rules and every special attack. |
| [Armour](/guide/items/armor) | Ancient, Ancient Royal, Nectric, Necros, God and Peaceful Warrior armour, and the Ancient Crown. |
| [Potions](/guide/items/potions) | Combat potions and travel potions. |
| [Utility items](/guide/items/utility-items) | Tomes, oaths, hymns, scrolls, flight items, build tools and more. |

Short swords no longer exist. A short sword you owned becomes the matching full sword: a Master Short Sword becomes a Master Sword,
a plain Iron Short Sword becomes an ordinary iron sword, and the Pwng Short Sword becomes the Pwng Sword.

Many custom items can be [repaired](/guide/mechanics/sacrificial-pits#repairs) at a sacrificial pit. Client feel tuning is deferred
to the final phase.
