# Ritual Tomb

Use `/warp ritual-site` to reach the soul campfire in Vineam. Punch the campfire to enter the tomb. If a ritual is already active, wait for it to finish. Punch the nearby skeleton skull to watch players in the tomb.

Throw valuables down the shaft at the center of the tomb. Their sacrifice value advances the ritual. Every 2,500 points raises its level, with more imps to defeat and more active at once. At level five, the cracked floor falls away. Watch your footing on the bridges.

The little Hallow imps fly toward the player with the highest percentage of health. Their hits leave at least one health until every participant falls below half health. When the title appears, they can kill.

Defeat the remaining imps to finish. A kill while holding a Pwng weapon also ends the ritual. Participants return to the campfire, where each player's reward is reserved for them. Offerings and kills contribute to the rewards. You keep your gear and experience if you die in the tomb.
