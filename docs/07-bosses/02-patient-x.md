# Patient X

Patient X (Glacies Mare district, under the frozen lake) is a mad zombie doctor with 1,000 health, and he is fast.

## Getting in

Stand in the Patient X theatre (Carpe Diem district) and sing the **Phantom Hymn** with 20 Phantom Essence; no other teleport gets
you in. Watch the fight from the skull in the theatre. No flying, and holy prayers fall silent there.

## Rage

Every hit makes him teleport somewhere else in the arena, and his **Rage** bar (3 to 9) sets how hard everything hits: his own blows
ignore armour. Hitting him raises his rage (a blaze rod in your hand raises it a lot); a **Calming Crystal** in your inventory keeps
it down, and hitting him with **Therapy Notes** (used up) calms him a lot.

## The arena

The ice over the water keeps freezing and thawing, sometimes throwing exploding snowballs; falling in hurts. His little patients and
nurses (baby zombies) keep pouring out of the middle and go for the nearest fighter, hiding or not; any that fall in heal him.

## Tricks

Every 20 seconds he plays a trick, announced by what he says:

* **Musical chairs:** everyone is teleported and their health scrambled.
* **A "smashing hit":** you drop to 3 health.
* **TNT**, **wither** and **splash potions**.
* **"Cold feet":** slowness.
* **Bats** that carry you off (sneak to let go).
* **Radiation:** stay out of the lit spots and the water while it lasts.
* **A snowball fight.**

## Dying and rewards

Dying there costs you nothing: your belongings come back when you respawn, but each death heals him and calms him back down. When he
falls, the fighters land in the middle and a drop party rains on the loot floor: sacrifice-style loot, a Nectric or Necros armour
piece, Therapy Notes and Odes to the Frozen King, and rarely a Calming Crystal. He returns ten minutes later.
