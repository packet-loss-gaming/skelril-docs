# Shnuggles Prime

Shnuggles Prime, the Giant Boss, is the first Skelril boss: a giant zombie trapped by the Forge Knights in a hall in the Vineam
district. It has 750 health (1,000 in a thunderstorm). It walks at the nearest player it can see and smashes everyone close by; its
hits throw you across the arena. Pressure plates by the doors let you in or out while it lives. No flying, and holy prayers fall
silent there.

## Baby zombies

Baby zombies pour out when you hit it, and the more of them there are, the more of your damage they absorb; with too many, your hits
heal it instead.

## Attacks

Every 15 seconds or so it uses one of nine attacks, announced by what it shouts:

| It shouts | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Taste my wrath!" | You are thrown and set alight. |
| "Embrace my corruption!" | Wither. |
| "Are you BLIND?" | Blindness. |
| "Tango time!" | Anyone it can see after 7 seconds is pulled in for a huge hit: hide behind a pillar. |
| "I am everlasting!" | For 15 seconds your hits heal it. |
| "turn up the heat" | Fire follows your feet. |
| "Bask in my glory!" | Strikes whoever it can see after 7 seconds. |
| The prayer to its lord | Strikes whoever it can see after 7 seconds. |
| "Sink into the darkness!" | Its zombies burst into deadly smoke. |

## Dying and rewards

Dying in the hall costs you nothing: your belongings come back when you respawn (no Gem of Life is used). Leaving or dying heals the
boss, so fight it together.

Each fighter who helps kill it gets their own loot, which only they can pick up: valuable sacrifice-style loot, gold, Barbarian
Bones, and with luck a Master weapon, a Magic Bucket, the Tattered Book or the Ancient Crown. Carry 3 to 16 Barbarian Bones into the
kill to offer them up and triple your loot (thunderstorms triple it too). The Tattered Book holds the knights' notes on its attacks,
and the boss fears it.

It comes back three minutes after it falls; watch the fight from the skull above the hall. Tested only without a game client so far.
