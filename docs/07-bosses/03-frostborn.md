# Frostborn

Frost Born, the Frozen King, is a giant snow golem with 1,750 health. He lives in a frozen hall in the Glacies Mare district. Use `/warp frostborn` to get there.

## Getting in

Drop down through the entrance. **The hall takes everything you carry, including your armour.** You arrive with nothing, and flight is switched off.
Your belongings wait for you until the king falls. Other teleports into the hall send you back to the gate.

Right-click a block with an **Ode to the Frozen King** at the gate to step inside past it. Right-click with it inside the building to step back out.
The Ode is not used up. Watch the fight from the skull by the gate.

## The fight

Left-click snow to pick up four snowballs. Your snowballs explode where they land, and the king's snowballs explode too.
Snow, snow blocks and glowstone on the floor break, but the floor rebuilds itself a few seconds later. You stay fed while you fight.

Every hit slows the king. Each hit also builds his rage, so the more you hit him, the sooner he uses a special attack:

* **"THIS ONE IS GOING TO HURT!"** throws one snowball that can start an avalanche from the ceiling.
* **"I AM SNOWTASTIC!"** opens snowball fountains across the floor.
* **"ME NO FEEL SO GOOD..."** makes him spew snowballs. Punching him causes this every time.
* **"RELEASE THE BATS!"** releases dozens of bats over the fighters, and each bat drops snowballs.

Every death heals him by a third. You keep your experience when you die.

## Rewards

When he falls, every fighter gets their own belongings back, but each item may be **duplicated or destroyed**. Items with "frozen" in their name are always safe.
Belongings left in the hall for more than a day drop for anyone who wins.
Each fighter also gets two stacks of Scrolls of Summation, plus chances at Hymns of Summation and Harvest and the City's tomes.
Solo and team victories count on the high scores. The king returns a minute later.

Tested only without a game client so far.
