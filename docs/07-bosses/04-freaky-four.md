# Freaky Four

Use `/warp freaky-four` to reach the arena in Oblitus. Bring a **Phantom Hymn**, at least **20 Phantom Essence**, a melee weapon and a bow. Right-click with the hymn at the entrance to start. After a boss falls, use the hymn again in that room to advance. Each room costs five essence to enter.

| Boss | What to watch for |
| --- | --- |
| Charlotte | Cave spider bites heal her. Her bite disables special attacks and armour bursts for 15 seconds. You can break cobwebs. |
| Frimus | Lava sweeps across the room, and a fireball takes half your maximum health. Projectiles cannot hurt him, so close in with a melee weapon. |
| Da Bomb | Hitting him moves both of you to new spots. If he finishes priming, everyone fighting in the room dies. |
| Snipee | Use a bow. Ordinary melee attacks cannot hurt him. An arrow takes half your maximum health, and the Tome of Divinity can sometimes deflect the shot. |

When Snipee falls, grab the loot from the double chest before lava closes in from both ends. Use the hymn again to return to the entrance. The chest can contain experience bottles, Phantom items, and Ancient or Ancient Royal armour.

You keep your inventory and experience on death. Flight is disabled in the arena. Punch the skeleton skull near the entrance to watch an occupied room.

The fight has been checked on the imported map in a headless server. Client playtesting is pending.
