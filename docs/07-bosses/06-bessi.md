# Bessi

Use `/warp cow-pasture` to reach the Carpe Diem pasture. After 15 cows die, the herd hides and Bessi appears. She is an enormous horned beast with 1,000 health.

Bessi calls pillagers, drops fireballs over places fighters have stood, and turns patches of the pasture into poisonous mycelium. Keep moving during the fireball volleys and leave purple ground as soon as it appears.

You can watch from the raised gallery. If you attack from there, you join the fight. Hit Bessi from the gallery and she pulls you down beside her and strikes.

Fighters keep their gear and experience if they die. Defeat Bessi for solo or team highscore credit and personal loot. Every fighter earns cooked beef and can roll God gear or combat potions.
