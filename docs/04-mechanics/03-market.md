# Market

The City market sells goods for Skrin. You can browse it from anywhere, but purchases and collection happen only inside the
City's bank market area in the Vineam district (`vineam-district-bank`).

## Browsing and buying

Run `/market` to browse the shop, or `/market <search>` to filter its items. Select an item, enter an amount and click **Buy**. The confirmation screen shows the total in Skrin before you pay. A price change requires refreshing the shop and confirming again.

Finish any mini-game before shopping. Existing items are preserved; you need room in your main inventory. Finite stock is shared by all players, while unlimited items stay available. Three percent of purchases contributes to the [lottery](/guide/mechanics/lottery).

## Collecting paid orders

If you move away, disconnect or fill your inventory during payment, your paid order stays saved. Return to the market, run `/market` and click **Collect paid orders**. An interrupted delivery may need an administrator to check it before collection can continue. The market does not automatically issue another copy when a previous delivery is uncertain.

## Stock and prices

Stock and prices update every two hours; expensive items retain their stock until bought.
