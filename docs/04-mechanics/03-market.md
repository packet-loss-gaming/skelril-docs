# Market

The City market trades through spirit goblin traders that drift about the market floor of the Vineam district bank,
around 592, 81, -1215. Every trader reaches the whole market; what a trader does decides which screen you get:

| Trader | Opens | How to recognise it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spirit Seller** | Buying | Blue. Wears a hood and a heavy backpack with a bedroll, holds out a tray of wares, wisps rise from its tail. |
| **Spirit Buyer** | Selling | Green. Wears a tall top hat and a monocle, holds out a coin purse, gold coins drop from it. |

Right-click (or hit) a trader to open its screen. Traders wander, but stop and wait while someone stands close. Trades and
collections only work while you stand near a trader.

## Browsing

Search for part of a name, then click a card to open the item's own page. A Spirit Seller's cards show what the item costs and how
much is left. A Spirit Buyer's cards show what the market pays for it first, then what it costs, and how many you carry. Every
item page shows both prices.

## Buying

At a Spirit Seller, open an item's page, enter an amount and click **Buy**. **+16**, **+32** and **+64** add to the amount;
pressed first, while it still says 1, they set it instead. The confirmation screen shows the total in Skrin before you pay. If the
price changed in the meantime, the purchase is refused and you look again.

Finish any mini-game before shopping. Existing items are preserved; you need room in your main inventory. Finite stock is shared by all players, while unlimited items stay available. Three percent of purchases contributes to the [lottery](/guide/mechanics/lottery).

## Selling

A Spirit Buyer opens a chest above your inventory. Put in whatever you want to sell, the same way as with any chest
(shift-click works). Only items the market buys go in. The panel beside it lists what is in the chest and what you will receive,
and it updates as you move things. **Add buyable from Backpack** moves everything sellable from your backpack (your inventory
except the hotbar) into the chest, and **Return all** empties it again. **Price list** shows every item the market buys with both prices; an item's page
can put all of that item in the chest.

**Closing the screen sells what is in the chest.** Anything the market cannot take at that moment comes back to your
inventory, and so does the whole chest if you disconnect with the screen open.

The market pays 80% of the current price, or 92% for items worth 100,000 Skrin or more. Worn tools and armor are paid in
proportion to their remaining durability. Enchanted items sell as the plain item. Renamed items and special items (such as
game equipment) are never sold. Selling adds to the shared stock.

The sale uses the prices at the moment you close the screen. If the market refuses part of it, for example because a price changed
at that moment, those items come back to you.

## Collecting paid orders

If you move away, disconnect or fill your inventory during payment, your paid order stays saved. Return to a Spirit Seller and click **Collect orders**. An interrupted delivery may need an administrator to check it before collection can continue. The market does not automatically issue another copy when a previous delivery is uncertain.

## Stock and prices

Stock and prices update every two hours; expensive items retain their stock until bought.
