# Skins

Your inventory's **Settings** tab has a wardrobe: every skin you own, with your **Default** look first. Tap a skin to wear it; everyone
sees the change at once. You can keep up to 10 skins.

## Adding a skin

Press **Add skin** and choose how to make it:

- **Build it in the skin editor.** Pick a base, eyes, hair, clothes and colours, starting from the skin you wear. Give it a name and
  press **Save**. **Cancel**, or closing the editor, throws your changes away.
- **Paste a skin file.** Use any skin made for Minecraft (a PNG of 64x64 or 64x32 pixels, at most 96 KB). The game takes it as text:
  open the [skin tool](/skins) in your browser, pick your file, press **Copy**, then paste the text into the game with Ctrl+V. The
  tool also shows your skin on a 3D model. The name is optional, and the game guesses whether the skin has slim arms; you can
  choose Classic or Slim yourself.

If you played on Grindstone, your Minecraft skin is already in your wardrobe and you are wearing it.

## Changing your skins

The buttons under the wardrobe act on the skin you are wearing:

- **Rename** gives it a new name.
- **Edit** opens a skin you built in the skin editor (or your Default look), with **Save** and **Cancel**.
- **Slim arms** switches a pasted skin between classic and slim arms.
- **Delete** takes it out of your wardrobe; you go back to your Default look.

Admins may remove a skin that is unsuitable for the server. You get a notice when they do, and you go back to your Default look.

## Heads

A Human Head that belongs to a player shows that player's skin: on the ground, on a wall, worn, and in your inventory. A head keeps
showing the skin it was made with, even after its owner deletes that skin.
