# Pitfall traps

Some floors in the City hide a **pitfall trap**: a thin sheet of purple-grey magic stone, buried one block under the floor with a drop
beneath it. Step onto the floor block above it and, a moment later, the floor and the trap under it vanish and you fall.
Every connected trap on the same level within 10 blocks opens with it, so a whole corridor can give way at once. Three
seconds later the floor is back as it was.

- Only standing on the block **above** a trap sets it off. Standing on an exposed trap sheet does nothing.
- Dropped items and mobs set traps off too, so a zombie can open a pit under you.
- A trap under a chest, a trapped chest or a shulker box stays shut, and so does a trap with nothing under it to fall into.
- Spectators and players in creative mode never set one off.
- Ninjas with **Pitfall Sneak** cross traps safely while sneaking. Rogues with **Pitfall Leap** leap away from a trap as it
  opens. See [Guilds](/guide/guilds).

::: info Not seen in a game client yet
Pitfall traps are tested on the server, but nobody has fallen into one in a client yet.
:::
