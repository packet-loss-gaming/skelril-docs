# Gates, lifts and other mechanisms

On Grindstone, gates, lifts, bridges and hidden doors were CraftBook signs. On Skelril they are real blocks,
placed where those signs used to be. A sign you write yourself, such as `[Gate]` or `[Lift Up]`, stays an
ordinary sign. Building your own mechanisms comes later.

You can use a mechanism wherever you may use things, for example open a gate or ride a lift. Sneak +
right-click shows what a mechanism does. Only staff can change its settings in the City.

::: info Not seen in a game client yet
These blocks are tested on the server, but nobody has looked at them in a client yet. Their look may
still change.
:::

## Gates

An iron **gate lever** hangs where the gate sign was. Right-click it to raise or lower the **portcullis**:
iron bars drop row by row from each fixed gate head down to the ground, and the lowest bar is spiked.
Opening pulls them back up. Some gates are also wired to redstone: then they close while the redstone is
on and open when it is off. A bar knocked out of a closed gate comes back the next time the gate closes.

## Bridges and doors

A **mystic anchor** sits where each end's old sign was. Right-click it and press the button to make the
bridge appear, a glowing walkway like a half slab, or to make it fade away again. It grows across block by
block and shrinks back the same way. Doors work the same way, as a glowing lattice that fills the doorway.
Some are wired to redstone and change each time the redstone changes.

## Coin tables

A **coin table** is an old relic that swaps a whole area between two states. One example is the door of
the Ninja guild. Right-click the table and press the dark redstone orb beside the state you want, or power the table with redstone
(a button, a lever or wire into the block): each time the power comes on, the area turns over.
The table's top turns over: green felt and a smiling coin in one state, pale oak and a frowning coin in the other. The
change spreads out from the table, with old blocks shrinking away and new ones growing into place. Some tables remember changes made to their area
before it turns.

## Lifts

**Lift panels** are brass plates on the wall:

- An arrow up takes you to the next panel straight above you. An arrow down takes you to the next panel
  below.
- A panel with a ring is a floor you can arrive at but not leave from.

You keep your position across the room and land on the floor under the panel you arrive at. If the floor
has a name, you see it when you arrive. A button on the other side of the wall from a panel works the lift
too.

## Hidden switches

Some walls hide a switch: right-clicking the right block flips a lever somewhere nearby. They look exactly
like the block around them.

## Circuits

The redstone machines of old builds (clocks, radio transmitters and receivers, sensors, logic gates, storm
rods, traps) are now **circuit blocks** with a glowing chip face. They drive the same levers and wiring as
before. Two of them are special:

- the **guild detector** at the Ninja guild opens its door for Ninjas;
- the **region portal** in Astaria carries everyone in one of two linked rooms to the other, one way at a time. Purple
  light runs down the walls of the room you leave and climbs the walls of the room you arrive in.
