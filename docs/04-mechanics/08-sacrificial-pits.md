# Sacrificial Pits

A **sacrificial pit** is a shallow pit floored with two layers of chiseled stone bricks (the City has one at the `SACRIFICIAL-PIT` warp, and the Grave
Yard's rewards room has a small one).

## Sacrificing

Throw items into a pit: the pit bursts into flames ("An ancient fire ignites."), and five seconds after your last
item the gods weigh everything you threw in together.

* Everything is worth its market value (less for worn tools). Worthless items come back; if nothing is worth anything, the gods reject your offer.
* The value becomes loot: mostly common things, now and then something valuable, and very rarely something legendary. Bigger offerings make the
  rare finds likelier. The total also counts toward the **Sacrificed Value** high score.
* Big offerings may bless you with prayers for an hour: Diggydiggy (haste), Health, Power, Speed, Antifire, Night Vision or Deadlydefense (arrows at
  nearby monsters).
* If your inventory is full, the gods keep the rest: **punch a chest** you may open to fill it. They keep it for you if you log out.
* `/sacrifice value` shows what the item in your hand is worth to the gods.
* A learned **Tome of Sacrifice** makes the valuable finds likelier, and a **Tome of Cleanliness** skips the junk.

## Special offerings

Phantom Gold pays 50 Skrin (100 in the rewards room's pit) and a Phantom Diamond 15,000 (18,000) straight into your wallet. A
Phantom Hymn turns into 20 Phantom Essence.

## Repairs

Some custom gear is repaired instead of sacrificed and comes straight back:

| Gear | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| Master weapons | Back to 80% at once. |
| Unleashed weapons | 6% per Imbued Crystal you carry. |
| Fear weapons | 6% per Gem of Darkness you carry. |
| Peaceful Warrior armour | 78% per bamboo you carry. |

Only as many repair items are used as needed. Repairs are twice as strong in the Grave Yard's rewards room. A nearly broken repairable item now and then
reminds you how to repair it.

## Building a pit

You can build your own pit in the worlds where you can build. A pit is a connected area of 24 to 400 open blocks (air or fire),
each with two chiseled stone bricks underneath it, so the smallest pit takes 48 bricks. Living things that step into a pit are
thrown back out.
