# Weather and the Apocalypse

## Weather

**Weather** follows a forecast: clear skies, rain and thunderstorms, each lasting 5 to 30 minutes. `/forecast` shows what is coming. A
thunderstorm never arrives out of a clear sky: a few minutes of rain always come first.

## The apocalypse

Every thunderstorm over the City is an **apocalypse**: a rift tears open somewhere in the City and the dead rise around it until the storm breaks.
You do not have to fight it, but you will want to know where it is.

* **The omen.** When the warning rain begins, the sky takes a red cast, distant thunder rolls in, a blood moon rises at night, and you are told
  which way the trouble lies: "An ill wind blows from the north-east... the sky over the Vineam District is turning red."
* **The storm.** "The dead are rising near the Vineam Prison in the Vineam District." A waypoint marks the rift and its distance. The closer you
  get, the redder and darker it becomes: fog closes in, ash falls, the edges of your view darken, and near the core your heart pounds.
* **The rift** towers over the epicenter, hums, and draws in the ash. Lightning strikes around it again and again, and every strike - there or
  anywhere near you in the storm - raises the dead somewhere around the epicenter. The ground around the rift corrupts as the storm goes on:
  grass becomes nylium and soul soil, stone becomes netherrack and blackstone, and flames flicker on it.
* **The horde** is many but simple-minded: it walks straight at the nearest player, hops small steps and strikes when close. Water does not stop it: the dead swim across and climb out on the far bank. They spread out
  as they rise and keep a little apart while they come for you. When several of
  you fight together, the dead split between you by who is nearest, so no one draws the whole horde.
* **When the storm breaks**, the dead fall, minibosses and all, the rift closes, and the corruption recedes until every block is as it was.

## Fighting the dead

Defeating the dead may strengthen one of four temporary storm buffs: Overlord, Magic Shield,
Damage Boost or Life Leach. `/buffs` lists your current levels under the Apocalypse category.
The server announces each increase as it happens. These buffs are memory-only and disappear
when the apocalypse ends.

Apocalyptic zombies sometimes drop gold, Phantom Ashes, and very rarely the Tome of the Rift Splitter or Peaceful Warrior
armour. Every kill may strengthen you for the rest of the storm: *Overlord* (your hits sometimes unleash Fear or Unleashed specials; Master
weapons do this too), *Magic Shield* (the dead hit softer), *Damage Boost* (you hit harder) and *Life Leach* (each kill heals you).
## Specialised zombies and minibosses

Watch for the stronger ones; each looks different from an ordinary zombie:

| Zombie | Looks like | Beware |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thor Zombie | Gold-plated, winged helm, white beard, golden pickaxe | Its hits fling you, then lightning strikes where you land. Explodes when it dies. |
| Zapper Zombie | Small, goggles, copper antennae and a coil on its back | Explodes into lightning when it hits you. It drops nothing special if it blows itself up. |
| Sticky Zombie | Bulky and wrapped in green slime | Its hits slow you to a crawl. |
| Chucker Zombie | Hunched, long arms, huge fists, a feathered shoulder | Throws other zombies at you. |
| Merciless Zombie | Very large, dark spiked armour, red eyes, golden sword | Forms when a hundred zombies chase one player. Eats other zombies to grow, quarters your health, and drags archers back. |
| Zombie Executioner | Black hood, leather straps, golden axe | Also forms from crowds. Stronger with every zombie near it, and heals by hitting you. |

The big ones drop gold for everyone who fought them, by how much damage each did; only you can pick up your share.

## Dying and other rules

If you die during a storm, the dead near your respawn point vanish and you come back stronger for a while. **Phantom Ashes** carry you into the
area around the rift while a storm rages. Weapons do not break blocks during a storm. The [Grave Yard](/guide/areas/graveyard) and the [Giant Boss](/guide/bosses/shnuggles-prime) hall are left alone.
