# Dying

Normal Minecraft death rules do not apply. What happens to your belongings depends on where you die.

## Graves

Without protection your belongings drop where you died. If you die in the City without a gem, your belongings are laid to rest in a
grave in the [Grave Yard](/guide/areas/graveyard): a chest under a headstone that now carries the date, "RIP" and your name. Most
valuable things go in first; what does not fit is dropped beside the grave if it is worth keeping, and lost otherwise.

Nobody else can open a grave until it is three days old, and old graves are reused first, so recover your things in time. Items
with the curse of vanishing never survive a death.

## Gems of Life

A **Gem of Life** in your inventory keeps everything you carry, including your experience, when you die: you get it all back when
you respawn and one gem is used up. A learned **Tome of Life** keeps your belongings without spending gems.

## Bosses, arenas and mini-games

Some places protect you on their own and never spend a gem: the [Sand Arena](/guide/areas/sand-arena), the
[Mirage Arena](/guide/areas/mirage-arena), the boss fights and the mini-games, which hand out their own gear.

## Getting back

**Phantom Potions** carry you to your most recent grave. For 30 seconds mobs leave you alone and the Grave Yard's fog cannot blind
you, until you hurt something. Without a known grave the potion is not drunk. New players get a **Newbie Phantom Potion** (see
[Getting started](/guide/getting-started)); it expires after the time shown on it.

The **Potion of Restitution** carries you to where you last died (with 10 seconds of poison); without a known death point it is not
drunk. Your belongings are in a grave, not where you died, so use a Phantom Potion to get them back.

You respawn at your [home](/guide/mechanics/warps#your-home) when it is safe, otherwise at the dimension's spawn.
