# Prayers

Prayers spend experience levels to bless or smite another online player for 15 minutes. Use
`/pray -l` to see the prayers your account may invoke and their level costs, then
`/pray <player> <prayer>` to use one.

Holy prayers include healing, speed, fire resistance, night vision, invisibility, absorption,
extra health, automatic defence and the combined Power, God, Flash and Hulk blessings. Unholy
prayers include fire, starvation, poison, blindness, slowness, smoke, explosions, hostile mobs,
falling arrows, inventory disruption and other mischief.

Some prayers are interactive. Fireball and God let the blessed player throw a fireball by
left-clicking the air, with a short cooldown. All other effects renew automatically while the
prayer remains active. Dying clears active prayers.

`/pray -c <player> <prayer>` clears that player's existing prayers first when the caller has
permission. Praying on yourself requires knowledge from the Tome of Divinity; otherwise the
gods answer with harmless lightning and reject the prayer. Administrators in admin mode do not
pay experience levels.
