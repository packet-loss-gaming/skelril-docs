# The Ninja Guild

Ninjas use precise movement, defensive tactics and archery to disorient and defeat their opponents. See [Guilds](/guide/guilds) for
joining, experience and commands.

## Base powers

| Power | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Arrow Bomb** | Left-click the air with a bow after firing: your arrow explodes where it is. |
| **Smoke Bomb** | Left-click a block with a bow: nearby enemies are pulled to where you stood, you swap places with the farthest one, and a bomb goes off at your old spot. |
| **Grapple** | Right-click a wall with an empty hand, sword or tool to climb to its top, up to 9 blocks. Sneak for a small hop instead. |
| **Free arrows** | One arrow in four comes back to you. |

## Advanced powers

| Level | Power | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | Extra Health | 30% more maximum health. |
| 3 | Graceful Falling | 20% less fall damage. |
| 5 | Pitfall Sneak | Sneak over pitfall traps without triggering them. |
| 7 | Fireproof | No damage from fire. |
| 12 | Multi-Arrow Bombs | Arrow Bomb detonates every arrow you fired within 5 seconds of your last one. |
| 16 | Potion Arrow Bombs | Tipped arrows leave a 10-second cloud of their potion instead of exploding. |
| 18 | Vampiric Smoke Bomb | Smoke Bombs heal you for each enemy caught. |
| 21 | Healing Arrows | Arrows that hit heal you, more the more hurt you are. |
| 25 | Ignition Special | A bow special attack (1 in 14 hits) that ignites the air along the arrow's path and keeps striking whatever it caught. |
| 30 | Master Climber | Grapple climbs up to 14 blocks. |
| 35 | Full Blooded Vampire | Stronger healing from Vampiric Smoke Bombs and Healing Arrows. |

## Ninja Parkour

The Ninja Parkour is in the Oblitus district. Stand on the stone bricks at the start and basalt columns rise out of the lava.
Cross to the stone bricks at the far end as fast as you can. Lava sends you back to the start, and the columns crumble when nobody
is playing. A crossing earns guild experience (ten times the base for a new record) and counts toward the Ninja Parkour
highscores. You cannot fly there.
