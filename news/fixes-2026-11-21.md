## Dying and respawning

Dying no longer crashes the server. Respawning brings you back as it should.

## Your ender chest is safe

Your ender chest is storage, not something you carry, and it is now treated that way everywhere. Dying no longer empties it into
your grave, the [Cursed Mine](/guide/areas/cursed-mine) no longer drains it when you die there, Frostborn no longer takes it with the
gear you leave at the door, and arenas and mini-games leave it alone instead of emptying it while you play. Gold Rush also no
longer asks you to empty your ender chest before you can enter. See [Dying](/guide/mechanics/death#graves).

## Phantom Potions

A [Phantom Potion](/guide/mechanics/death#getting-back) drunk in Astaria or on Halzeil used to drop you out of the sky over a random
part of the City instead of carrying you to your grave. It now takes you to the Grave Yard from any world. The Phantom Clock had the
same problem and is fixed too.

## Jumping

A single jump no longer carries you up two full-block steps at once. Stepping onto a ledge now only happens while you are on the
ground or falling, which feels much closer to Minecraft.

## The apocalypse

* The dead can swim. Water no longer stops the horde: zombies rise to the surface, swim across and climb out on the far bank.
* Zombies no longer spawn stacked on top of each other. They rise spread out around each lightning strike and keep a little apart
  while they come for you.

## On fire

The flames across your screen while you burn are animated again instead of frozen on a single frame.

## Patient X

The radiation attack shows green radiation glitter on the irradiated spots, which were missing their effect before.

## Bubble columns

Bubble columns are back. Water standing on soul sand carries you, mobs and items up, and water standing on magma drags them
down, with bubbles rising or sinking through the column. While you are inside one you never run out of breath. In the
[Factory](/guide/areas/factory) this brings the fountain back to life: potions and drowned come spewing out of it again
instead of sitting at the bottom.

## Ore pools

Ore pools on [Halzeil](/guide/areas/halzeil#world-levels) no longer sound like a blaze. Each burst now clatters out of the
rock with a rush of hot air and a heavy thud under a low ring, pitched to the ore (deeper for coal, brighter for diamond). The
first burst is the highest and each one after it sinks a little as the vein runs out. Fragments of the ore fly out of the hole
while embers and ash drift up from it, and a final thud and ring tells you when the pool has run dry. Pacing and drops are
unchanged.

## Your inventory

* The off-hand slot is gone: Luanti has no real dual wielding, so whatever you hold is in your main hand. Anything you had in the
  off-hand is moved into your inventory the next time you join.
* The Help button is replaced by **Warps**, which opens your warp menu, and the trophy now opens the **Hall of Records** with every
  high score table. `/warps` and `/highscores` still work. See [Your inventory](/guide/getting-started#your-inventory).
* Items from the starter kit and from restored Grindstone inventories, such as chests, doors and signs, now arrive as the normal
  items instead of odd placed-block versions.

## Torches from your pickaxe

An old Skelril favourite is back: right-click a block while holding any pickaxe and a torch from your inventory goes on the face you
clicked, on the floor or on a wall. Torches, soul torches and copper torches all work. Chests and doors still open as usual, and
sneaking keeps the pickaxe's own right-click.

## Guild powers

Your guild powers are on whenever you log in. If you log in somewhere they are not allowed, such as jail or an arena, they switch on
as soon as you leave.

## Linear Block Placer

The Linear Block Placer now places blocks by itself and has a settings screen. Right-click the air (or sneak and right-click) to
choose the block it lays and the distance, from 1 to 9. Then right-click a block face to lay a line of that block, taken from your
inventory. The settings are saved on the item. See [utility items](/guide/items/utility-items).
