The Ritual Tomb is open in Vineam. Use `/warp ritual-site` and punch the soul campfire. Down below, throw offerings into the central shaft to wake the little flying imps of Hallow.

The imps go after whoever has the highest percentage of health. Reach level five and the cracked floor gives way, so keep an eye on the bridges as you fight.
