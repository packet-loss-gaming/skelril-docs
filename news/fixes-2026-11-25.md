## Name tags and line of sight

Your name above your head now follows the fight, which matters most in stealthy games like
[Jungle Raid](/guide/minigames/jungle-raid):

* Outside PvP, everyone sees your name, as before.
* In PvP (with `/pvp` on or in a PvP area), in a Jungle Raid or Sky Wars arena, and while you sneak, other players only see your
  name while they have a clear line of sight to you. Solid blocks and leaves hide it; glass, fences, slabs and plants do not.
* Your team mates always see your name, wherever you are, and spectators see everyone's.
* Invisible players show no name at all.

Before, every name showed through walls at any distance. See [Player combat](/guide/protecting-your-stuff#player-combat).

## Mirage Arena

* The map vote now shows on a copper plaque at the top of the screen: the map that would win right now, the seconds left and the
  other maps' votes. While the arena rebuilds, the same plaque shows the map, how far along it is and whether it is draining
  water and lava or putting blocks back. The old list at the right edge of the screen is gone. See
  [Voting for a map](/guide/areas/mirage-arena#voting-for-a-map).
* Hitting a player who had just died no longer kills them a second time. That second death left the player stuck until they
  reconnected to get their belongings back.

## Dying

* Dying in the [Mirage Arena](/guide/areas/mirage-arena) keeps everything you carry, and your experience, for your respawn, and
  never spends a Gem of Life. Before, your belongings were sent to the Grave Yard, gem or no gem.
* Behind the scenes, every place that protects your belongings on death now goes through one system with a fixed order, so
  arenas, boss fights, mini-games, gems and graves can no longer get in each other's way. See [Dying](/guide/mechanics/death).
* You respawn in exactly one place: your cell if you are jailed, the arena's exit if an arena sends you somewhere, otherwise your
  home or the spawn. No more jumping between spots right after respawning.
* Dying on a [Spleef](/guide/minigames/spleef) floor no longer drops the spleef shovel for someone else to pick up.

## Explosions follow build rights

TNT you light or place, beds and respawn anchors you use, and guild explosives (Ninja arrow bombs, the Rogue's grenade and Meteor)
now break exactly the blocks you could build on yourself, and nothing else. Quarrying with TNT works anywhere you may build,
including high up in Halzeil. Creepers and other monsters still cannot blow holes in protected places, the City, Astaria or
Halzeil's surface, though their blasts still hurt.

## Phantom Potions bring a pickaxe

A [Phantom Potion](/guide/mechanics/death#getting-back) now hands you a wooden pickaxe when you arrive at your grave, if you are not
already carrying one, so you can dig your belongings out straight away.

## Rogues land softly

[Rogues](/guide/guilds/rogue) take 30% less fall damage. **Impact** is now the safe way down from great heights, as it was always
meant to be: you take 60% less damage than the fall itself would deal, and never more than three hearts. Slamming down no longer
costs anything extra, so diving off a tower or Astaria's edge with Impact is a short sting rather than a death sentence.
**Like a Meteor**'s ring of explosions no longer hurts you either.

## Ninjas

**Grapple** no longer climbs when you click right next to a door, a button or another usable block, or on the wall behind one.
See [Ninja](/guide/guilds/ninja).

## Jungle Raid and Sky Wars

* You now start the [Jungle Raid](/guide/minigames/jungle-raid) on solid ground. Before, almost a third of the starting spots left
  you in mid-air above the jungle.
* Walking through the lobby door of Jungle Raid or [Sky Wars](/guide/minigames/sky-wars) no longer leaves the wrong item in your
  hand. Going in used to copy the item you held, and coming out could replace a real item with one from the arena kit.

## Arenas and the jail

* Blocks that fall, such as sand and gravel, can no longer be placed in the [Sand Arena](/guide/areas/sand-arena) by putting them
  against the side of a block.
* Each prisoner gets a jail cell of their own while the prison has an empty one, and nobody lands inside the cell bars any more.

## Tools and items

* The linear and radial digging tools no longer dig a trail of lines or squares when you sweep the mouse while holding the button:
  each tool fires at most four times a second, and the Linear Block Placer lays at most ten lines a second. You now also hear and
  see every block your tool breaks.
* Drawing a bow or crossbow is smooth: the bow no longer dips out of view at each stage of the draw.
* Dropped items no longer catch your clicks: you can open a door or a chest, or dig the block behind them, without picking them up
  first.
* Command replies are coloured: yellow when a command worked, red when it did not.

## Shields are gone

With no off-hand there is no dual wielding, so shields are removed from the game. The Knight and Engineer kits in Jungle Raid no
longer include one, vaults and villagers no longer offer them, and shields from Minecraft-era inventories are not restored.

## Fire in the City

Fire no longer spreads or burns blocks away in the City, Astaria or Halzeil, except where an arena allows it (the Mirage Arena and the
Jungle Raid). Lava no longer sets anything alight.

## Mystic bridges

The drifting sparks over mystic bridges and doors no longer show dark squares when you see them against the bridge. The same fix
applies to the region portal's sparks.
