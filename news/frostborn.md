Frost Born, the Frozen King, has returned to his hall in the Glacies Mare district (`/warp frostborn`). The hall takes everything
you carry at the door, armour included, and keeps it safe until the king falls. Inside, pick up snowballs from the floor: they
explode where they land, and so do his.

Every hit slows him down and builds his rage, until he answers with avalanches from the ceiling and fountains of snowballs. Solo
and team victories count on the high scores. An Ode to the Frozen King lets you step past the gate and back out again.
