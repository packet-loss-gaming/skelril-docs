## Notice cards

What the game tells you on its own now arrives as a card at the bottom left of the screen instead of a line in chat: special
attacks, bosses' moves (Patient X, Shnuggles Prime and the apocalypse's minibosses), apocalypse warnings, Cursed Mine hauntings, the
lottery, the jail, drop parties and Goblet winners. Each card has a coloured edge and an icon by kind, and boss cards show the boss's
face.

* Up to five cards stack in the corner. The newest sits on top and shows the whole message; the ones under it shrink to one line.
* Warnings of a coming attack flash their edge and play a short magical sting, so you can react without reading.
* Chat keeps what players say and the replies to your commands. Cards are not copied into chat, so read them while they are up.

See [Messages from the game](/guide/getting-started#messages-from-the-game).

## Minecarts

* A minecart that leaves its rail, or loses the rail under it, now falls, and settles back onto a rail if it lands on one. Carts no
  longer hang in mid-air.
* Punch a cart to break it, as in classic Minecraft: a few quick punches do it, and it shakes with each one. Breaking or picking up a
  cart needs build rights where it stands. A rider's punches only push.

## Hearts in rows

Health above ten hearts, from the Ninja's Extra Health or a Health Boost potion, now stacks in extra rows of hearts above the health
bar instead of squeezing the bar. When that extra health ends, it no longer plays a hurt sound, kicks you out of bed or makes you
hungrier.

## Combat

* Special attacks can now trigger on the blow that kills a target. Before, only hits the target survived could roll one. See
  [Special attacks](/guide/items/weapons#special-attacks).
* Where your falls do no harm (Sky Wars, for one), landing no longer flashes red or plays a hurt sound. Bouncing off jungle leaves in
  the [Jungle Raid](/guide/minigames/jungle-raid) goes *boing*.
* Monsters in range worlds no longer burst into a cloud of thousands of hurt particles when you hit them.

## The apocalypse

When the storm breaks, every one of its dead falls, minibosses included; none linger after the sky clears. Minibosses now swim like the
rest of the horde. A crash that could take the server down while minibosses died is fixed. See
[the apocalypse](/guide/mechanics/apocalypse).

## Crossbows and tridents

Luanti can only draw the item in your hand at the right of the screen, which makes aiming a crossbow or a trident feel wrong, so
neither is a player item for now. Pillagers and piglins still shoot crossbows and drowned still throw tridents, but they never drop
them, they cannot be crafted, and vaults, villagers and loot no longer offer them. Enchanted books no longer carry their
enchantments (Multishot, Piercing, Quick Charge, Loyalty, Riptide, Channeling and Impaling). Crossbows from Minecraft-era
inventories are not restored. We would like to bring the crossbow back once Luanti can show it properly.

## Removed items

* The **recovery compass**, the **mace** (with Density, Breach and Wind Burst) and the **brush** are gone. Suspicious sand and
  gravel stay as blocks, and the Heavy Core stays as a decorative block.
* The **screwdriver** is gone.
* The **respawn anchor** is gone. Your respawn point is set by your home warp, as before.
* **Command blocks** and the command block minecart are gone.
