The City market has new staff. Spirit goblins drift about the Vineam bank's market floor: blue, hooded **Spirit Sellers** with a
pack full of wares sell you goods, and green **Spirit Buyers** in top hats with a coin purse buy them. Right-click one to trade;
every trader reaches the whole market, and they stop and wait while you stand close. The `/market` command is gone.

Browsing works like the warp menu: search, then pick an item card for its own page, which shows both what the item costs and what
the market pays. When buying, **+16**, **+32** and **+64** add to the amount.

A Spirit Buyer opens a chest: put in what you want to sell, or let **Add buyable from Backpack** fill it, watch the total add up beside it,
and close the screen to sell. The market pays 80% of the current price (92% for the most valuable goods), and worn tools are paid
by their remaining durability.

The market comes with a new look for every screen, from your inventory to chests and furnaces: the Dwarven Forge, soot-black iron
plates in a copper frame with copper-inlaid buttons. The high scores moved into a book of their own too: `/highscores` opens the
**Hall of Records**.
