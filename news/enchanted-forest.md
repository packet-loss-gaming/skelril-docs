The Enchanted Forest is back in Carpe Diem. Take `/warp enchanted-forest`, pick a tree and start chopping: every log pays
experience and can drop up to three pieces of loot. Chopping is hungry work, so bring food.

The forest is not entirely friendly. Fairies play tricks, from a dose of poison to a scattered inventory, and clearing the
grass can turn up spawn eggs, a rabbit or, now and then, the Killer Bunny. If it shows up, get out of the grass.

The trees grow back after three hours, so there is always another stand to clear.
