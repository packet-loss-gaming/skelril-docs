---

The Cursed Mine has reopened in the Oblitus district. Bring a pickaxe: the ore yields extra resources and experience, and
it grows back about 30 seconds after you mine it. The deepest level holds emeralds, guarded by blazes.

The mine wants its valuables back and slowly drains them from your inventory, unless the item has a name. Mining can wake a
ghost. Some bring gifts; others put you on Dave's hit list, and then warps, portals and pearls won't get you out.
