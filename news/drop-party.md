summary: A weekly party in the Oblitus Party Room on Saturdays at 20:00, or host your own.
---

The Oblitus Party Room is open again. Every Saturday at 20:00, server time, a countdown starts and party boxes rain down
for anyone in the room. Take `/warp party-room` and watch chat for the announcement.

You don't have to wait for Saturday. Fill the room's chests with anything you want to give away, press a stone button,
and after a one-minute countdown the whole lot drops. Run into a falling box or grab it once it lands; it bursts open
on the spot, and anyone can collect what's inside.
