Vineam Prison is open for raids. Take `/warp VINEAM-PRISON` and find your way through the cell blocks to the chest in the
office. It pays gold and bonus loot, then sends you back to the entrance to try again.

No shortcuts: flight, guild powers and holy prayers stop working inside, and pearls and other teleports between rooms are
blocked. You have to walk the maze.
