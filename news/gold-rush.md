The Vineam bank has a weak spot. Right-click a **Play Gold Rush** sign nearby, bring an empty inventory and at least 100
Skrin, and press the lobby button to start a seven-minute heist.

Hunt the first room's chests for the red and blue wool keys and fit each to its lock. Next comes the lever room: watch the
flashes, match the pattern, and do it before the water rises. Carry a Phantom Hymn and the flood is lava instead.

Reach the treasury chest to escape with your split, paid straight into your wallet. Dying on the job costs you, so plan
your route.
