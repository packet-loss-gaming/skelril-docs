## Dying

* Dying in the [Mirage Arena](/guide/areas/mirage-arena) keeps everything you carry, and your experience, for your respawn, and
  never spends a Gem of Life. Before, your belongings were sent to the Grave Yard, gem or no gem.
* Behind the scenes, every place that protects your belongings on death now goes through one system with a fixed order, so
  arenas, boss fights, mini-games, gems and graves can no longer get in each other's way. See [Dying](/guide/mechanics/death).
* You respawn in exactly one place: your cell if you are jailed, the arena's exit if an arena sends you somewhere, otherwise your
  home or the spawn. No more jumping between spots right after respawning.
* Dying on a [Spleef](/guide/minigames/spleef) floor no longer drops the spleef shovel for someone else to pick up.

## Phantom Potions bring a pickaxe

A [Phantom Potion](/guide/mechanics/death#getting-back) now hands you a wooden pickaxe when you arrive at your grave, if you are not
already carrying one, so you can dig your belongings out straight away.

## Rogues land softly

[Rogues](/guide/guilds/rogue) take 30% less fall damage. **Impact** is now the safe way down from great heights, as it was always
meant to be: you take 60% less damage than the fall itself would deal, and never more than three hearts. Slamming down no longer
costs anything extra, so diving off a tower or Astaria's edge with Impact is a short sting rather than a death sentence.
**Like a Meteor**'s ring of explosions no longer hurts you either.

## Jungle Raid

You now start the [Jungle Raid](/guide/minigames/jungle-raid) on solid ground. Before, almost a third of the starting spots left you
in mid-air above the jungle.

## Shields are gone

With no off-hand there is no dual wielding, so shields are removed from the game. The Knight and Engineer kits in Jungle Raid no
longer include one, vaults and villagers no longer offer them, and shields from Minecraft-era inventories are not restored.

## Fire in the City

Fire no longer spreads or burns blocks away in the City, Astaria or Halzeil, except where an arena allows it (the Mirage Arena and the
Jungle Raid). Lava no longer sets anything alight.

## Mystic bridges

The drifting sparks over mystic bridges and doors no longer show dark squares when you see them against the bridge. The same fix
applies to the region portal's sparks.
