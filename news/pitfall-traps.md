---

Pitfall traps are back. A thin sheet of magic stone hides one block under some of the City's floors, with a drop beneath it. Step
onto the floor above one and, a moment later, the floor and the trap vanish and you fall. Connected traps open together, so a
whole corridor can give way at once, and three seconds later the floor is back as if nothing happened.

Mobs and dropped items set traps off too. Ninjas with **Pitfall Sneak** can cross them safely while sneaking, and Rogues with
**Pitfall Leap** leap away as one opens.
