summary: Healing pools in Carpe Diem and Glacies Mare, and a geyser that launches you skyward.
---

The City's hot springs are flowing in Carpe Diem and Glacies Mare. A dip clears poison, nausea, blindness, weakness and
slowness, and sends you off with Regeneration, Speed and Jump Boost. The Tome of Divinity makes the effects last twice as
long.

At Carpe Diem, step onto the lapis geyser for a ride straight up, with a safe landing included. Just keep an eye on the
weather: in a thunderstorm, zombies can climb out of the pools.
