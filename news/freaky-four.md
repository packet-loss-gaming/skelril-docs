The Freaky Four are back in the Oblitus arena. Use `/warp freaky-four` and bring a Phantom Hymn, 20 Phantom Essence, a melee weapon and a bow.

Fight through Charlotte's webs, Frimus's lava walls, and Da Bomb's room-wide blast. Snipee guards the reward chest, and once he falls you have to loot it before the lava reaches you.
