summary: Fly and push your rivals out of the Vineam sky with Sky Feathers, and shoot chickens for power-ups.
---

Join through the Sky Wars lobby door in Vineam, stand on a glass deck for your team and get launched into the sky. Your Sky
Feather flies on a left-click and blasts a flame line on a right-click that pushes enemies away.

Chickens drifting through the arena hide power-ups: the Book o' Omens, Frost Orbs, Defrosters, stronger feathers and, very
rarely, the Infinite Doom feather. Stay out of the water and be the last one flying.
