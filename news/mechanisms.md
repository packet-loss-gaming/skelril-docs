type: feature
title: Gates, lifts and bridges are real blocks
date: 2026-11-25
guide: /guide/mechanics/mechanisms
summary: The old CraftBook signs are gone. Portcullis gates, mystic bridges, lift panels, coin tables and circuits now stand where they used to be.
---

Every gate, lift, bridge, hidden door and redstone contraption that used to be a CraftBook sign is now a real block in the same
place. Pull a **gate lever** and the iron portcullis drops row by row. Press a **mystic anchor** and a glowing bridge grows across
the gap. Step to a brass **lift panel** to ride up or down to the next floor. Flip a **coin table** and a whole area turns over,
like the door of the Ninja guild.

The old redstone machines (clocks, radio links, sensors and traps) are now **circuit blocks** with a glowing chip face, and they
still drive the same wiring. Sneak and right-click any mechanism to see what it does.

Building your own mechanisms comes later, when they become craftable.
