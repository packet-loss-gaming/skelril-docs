date: 2026-11-20

summary: The Carpe Diem jungle battle returns, with six classes, four modes, eighteen flags and a titan to hunt.
---

Step through the Jungle Raid lobby door in Carpe Diem, pick a class on the signs and stand on the white, blue or red concrete
with at least one rival. After a calm minute in the jungle, the last player or team standing wins.

Turn the flags on before you start: TNT rain, exploding arrows, grenades from the sky, Death Touch, or a titan with night
vision and double damage that passes to whoever brings it down. Kills and long shots earn points, and points pay Skrin.
