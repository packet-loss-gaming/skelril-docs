title: Falling out of Astaria
date: 2026-11-19
guide: /guide/areas/sky-world
summary: Fall through the bottom of the sky world and you reappear high above the City.
---

Fall off the edge of Astaria and you no longer disappear into the void. You reappear high above the City and keep
falling, so the trip down is part of the fun.

Land carefully, since the fall can still hurt.
