# Skin tool

Pick a skin file (a PNG of 64x64 pixels, or the older 64x32, as made for Minecraft) and press **Copy**. Then paste the text into the
game: open your inventory, **Settings**, **Add skin**, **Paste a skin file**. Your file never leaves your computer.
See [Skins](/guide/mechanics/skins) for the wardrobe.
